From Lyssa · ecstatic feminine force

Derived from Lyssa, representing ecstatic feminine force, instinct, and liberation. Lyssara carries embodied wisdom, intuition over intellect, emotional truth without apology, and healer energy that works through presence and resonance. It is not chaos. It is wild intelligence.

Lyssara feels like the woman who trusts her knowing. The one who listens beneath words. The healer who does not chase, she attracts.

Nyx

Nyx is the primordial goddess of Night, the original womb of creation. She carries deep intuition, sovereignty, protection, and the stillness before becoming. Nyx energy is ancient authority without force.
